What it does

Music and video in one place

Movies and shows from Jellyfin, music from Jellyfin or Navidrome/Subsonic. No need for two or three separate apps.

Plays everything

Built on mpv. MKV, HEVC, AV1, DTS, TrueHD, ASS and PGS subtitles, FLAC, gapless. Direct play whenever possible.

Works away from home

Connects over Tailscale out of the box. No port forwarding, and it doesn't care if your ISP puts you behind CGNAT.

Offline and on the road

Downloads for flights and bad coverage, a data-saver mode, and Android Auto for music. planned

No account, no ads, no tracking

The app talks to your server and nothing else. This page has no analytics either.

Pay once

Free core player. Extra features as a one-time purchase, not another subscription.
