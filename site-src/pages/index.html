Everything on your server, in your pocket

Movies and shows from Jellyfin, music from Jellyfin or Navidrome, photos both ways. One app instead of two or three.

Music, done properly

A home for your music: recently added, recently played, most played and favorites. Instant Mix plays songs like the one you love. Lyrics follow the song, line by line. Albums play without gaps, and you see the real format: FLAC · 24-bit · 96 kHz. The queue is still there after a restart.

Plays every file

Built on mpv: MKV, HEVC, AV1, DTS, Dolby, FLAC and styled ASS subtitles, as they are, no waiting for the server to convert.

Plays through bad coverage

While you listen, the next songs are saved on the phone, so the music goes on in tunnels, lifts and on the train. On mobile data, choose 320, 192 or 128 kbps and save your plan.

Share your server with family and friends

Parents in another city, a friend across town: send them a link, and your movies and music are on their phone. No accounts to create, no passwords to explain.

  1. Pick what to share. Choose the libraries. Your photos stay private unless you add them.
  2. Send the link in any messenger. It works once, for one person, for 7 days.
  3. They tap “Add to OwnMedia”. No name, no password. They can watch and listen, but can’t delete anything or pass the access on.

You stay in control

“Who has access” shows each person’s phone, when they last watched and what’s on now. “End access” closes it at once, and their app tells them so.

Questions

Do I need my own server?

Yes. OwnMedia plays what is on your Jellyfin or Navidrome server; it doesn’t sell or stream any content of its own. No server yet? Any Windows PC that stays on can be one: the OwnMedia installer sets it up in a few clicks. A NAS works too.

What is Jellyfin?

A free, open-source media server. It sorts the movies, shows, music and photos in your folders, fetches posters and descriptions, and serves them to apps like OwnMedia. Navidrome is the same idea for music only. OwnMedia isn’t made by either project.

Do I need Tailscale?

Not at home: there, the phone talks to the server over your Wi-Fi. To watch away from home, the Windows installer can set up Tailscale for you (a free service; you sign in once in the browser). If you already reach your server another way, a VPN or your own domain, OwnMedia uses that. More about remote access.

What happens to my data?

It stays between your phone and your server. There is no OwnMedia account, and the app sends us nothing about you: no analytics, no crash reports, no ads. The app from this site asks ownmedia.app once a day whether there is a new version; you can turn that off. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I get new versions?

The app tells you when a new version is out (Settings → Updates). Tap to download it and install it over the old one: your servers, downloads and settings stay. The newest version is always at ownmedia.app/download.

Can I watch on my TV?

Yes. “Play on TV” sends a movie or an episode to most smart TVs on your Wi-Fi (they accept video from phones over UPnP/DLNA), and your phone becomes the remote. There is no separate app for Android TV, LG or Samsung TVs. How it works.

Who else can see my server?

Only the people you invite. “Share with friends” in the app makes an invite link for the libraries you choose; it works once, for 7 days. The friend adds your server with one tap, with no password, and can only watch and listen. Under “Who has access” you see each friend’s phone, when they last watched and what they’re watching now, and “End access” closes their access at once. How invites work.
