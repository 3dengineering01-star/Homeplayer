Guides

Set up the server once, then everything happens in the app. Pick where your movies and music live.

{{icon:windows}}

Windows PC

One installer: Jellyfin, your folders, access from the internet and a QR code for the phone. The quickest start.
{{icon:nas}}

Synology NAS

Jellyfin in Container Manager, the OwnMedia plugin from the catalogue, Tailscale for the internet. About half an hour.
{{icon:box}}

Docker on Linux or another NAS

The same compose file and plugin catalogue, for any machine that runs Docker.
{{icon:tv}}

Watch on your TV

Send a movie from the phone to a smart TV on your Wi-Fi and use the phone as the remote.
{{icon:globe}}

Away from home and friends

How OwnMedia reaches your server over the internet, how to make it faster, how invites for friends work and how to end someone’s access.
