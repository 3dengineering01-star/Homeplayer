Support

Something not working, or an idea? Write to us. A real person reads every message.

Email

support (at) ownmedia.app

We answer in English or Russian.

What to send when something goes wrong

  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • The OwnMedia version: Android Settings → Apps → OwnMedia.
  • Your server: Jellyfin or Navidrome and its version; Windows, NAS or Docker.
  • For a video that won’t play: the file’s format if you know it (for example MKV, HEVC, DTS), or what Jellyfin shows about it under Media info.
  • A screenshot, if there is an error message.
  • Problems with the Windows installer: attach C:\ProgramData\Homeplay\setup.log. It never contains your password.

Please don’t send your password or the invite links you made: we never need them.
