# Страница в Google Play

Основной язык — английский. Переводы: русский, немецкий, французский, испанский (те же языки, что на сайте).
Текст простыми словами, без технических терминов (Tailscale, кодеки, VPN): его читает человек, который не
разбирается в серверах. Чужие названия приложений и сервисов (Netflix, Spotify) не упоминать: правила Google Play.
Ограничения Google Play: название до 30 символов, краткое описание до 80, полное до 4000.

Название «Jellyfin» в заголовке не ставим: это чужой товарный знак, а в описании упоминать серверы можно.

## English (en-US)

**Название** (27): `OwnMedia: home media player`

**Краткое описание** (80): `Your movies, music and photos from your home server. No subscriptions, no cloud.`

**Полное описание** (2557):

```
OwnMedia plays your movies, shows, music and photos straight from your home server. Everything stays with you: no subscriptions to someone else's service, and your files and data go nowhere else.

WHAT YOU NEED
A computer or a network storage drive with the free Jellyfin or Navidrome program. No server yet? An ordinary Windows computer becomes one in a few minutes: the program from ownmedia.app sets everything up for you.

WATCH AND LISTEN TO ALMOST ANYTHING
• Movies in most formats start right away
• Subtitles and several audio tracks (the app remembers your choice)
• Pick between versions of a movie (for example, 4K or regular)

WORKS AWAY FROM HOME
Watch and listen to your server from anywhere. The Windows setup program opens access over the internet by itself: no router settings.
On a slow connection, "Auto" picks a quality that keeps the video going. On mobile data you can lower the quality and save your data plan.

DOWNLOAD FOR THE ROAD
Download movies, episodes, whole seasons or albums and watch without internet. When you're back online, your server learns where you stopped.

PHOTOS FROM YOUR PHONE STRAIGHT TO YOUR SERVER
New photos and videos are saved to your server a few seconds after you take them. No cloud, no monthly fee (needs a free add-on for the server). Browse your photos in a grid, full screen, with zoom.

MUSIC
The home screen shows what you added recently, what you play most and your favorites. Similar songs start with one tap. Lyrics follow the music, albums play without gaps, and your queue is kept. There's an equalizer, volume levelling and a sleep timer.
On a bad connection the next songs are saved to the phone in advance, so the music keeps playing on the subway and out of town.

SHARE WITH FAMILY AND FRIENDS
Send a link in any messenger. They install the app and join your server with one tap, no login or password. You see who joined and can end their access with one button.

ON THE TV AND IN THE CAR
Send a movie to a TV on your Wi-Fi and use your phone as the remote. Android Auto: your music in the car, with voice search.

30 DAYS FREE, THEN ONE PURCHASE
For the first 30 days everything is free. After that, OwnMedia Pro: a one-time purchase in Google Play, not a subscription. Friends you invite use it for free when you have Pro.

NO STRINGS
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. The app talks only to your server. Lyrics that your server doesn't have are fetched from the free LRCLIB collection (you can turn this off).

OwnMedia is an independent app, not affiliated with Jellyfin or Navidrome.
```
